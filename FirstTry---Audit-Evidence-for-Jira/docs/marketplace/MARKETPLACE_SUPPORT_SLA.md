# Support Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-08  
**Version:** 2.0.0

## 1. Overview

This document defines the support services available for the App and associated response times.

## 2. Support Channels

### 2.1 Email Support

- **Email:** support@firsttry.run
- **Response Time:** See Section 4

### 2.2 Issue Tracker

- **URL:** https://github.com/example/repo/issues
- **For:** Bug reports, feature requests

### 2.3 Documentation

- **Location:** docs/marketplace/ directory
- **Self-service:** Available 24/7

## 3. Support Hours

### 3.1 Standard Support

- **Hours:** Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM UTC
- **Holidays:** Reduced support

### 3.2 After-Hours Support

- **Critical Issues Only:** May have longer response times
- **Escalation:** Via email with "[URGENT]" prefix

## 4. Response Time Targets

Response times are **targets**, not guarantees. Actual response times may vary.

### 4.1 Severity Definitions

**Critical (P1):**
- App completely unavailable
- Data loss occurring
- Security vulnerability

**High (P2):**
- Major functionality broken
- Significant performance degradation
- Many users affected

**Medium (P3):**
- Feature not working as expected
- Minor functionality issue
- Workaround available

**Low (P4):**
- Feature requests
- Documentation questions
- Cosmetic issues

### 4.2 Target Response Times

| Severity | Target First Response | Target Resolution |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| P1 (Critical) | 4 business hours | Best effort |
| P2 (High) | 1 business day | Best effort |
| P3 (Medium) | 2 business days | Best effort |
| P4 (Low) | 3 business days | Best effort |

**Note:** These are response time targets, not resolution guarantees.

## 5. Scope of Support

### 5.1 Included Support

We provide support for:
- App installation issues
- Configuration guidance
- Bug reports and fixes
- Feature clarifications

### 5.2 Excluded from Support

We do NOT provide support for:
- Custom development requests
- Jira administration (contact Atlassian)
- Third-party integrations
- Network/infrastructure issues

## 6. Support Lifecycle

### 6.1 Active Support

- **Current Version:** Fully supported
- **Previous Version (N-1):** Bug fixes only
- **Older Versions:** End of support

### 6.2 End of Life (EOL)

Apps may be designated EOL with:
- 90 days advance notice
- Migration guidance provided
- Critical security fixes during transition

## 7. Escalation Process

### 7.1 Standard Escalation

1. Submit issue via email or tracker
2. Wait for initial response (per SLA)
3. If no response, re-send with "[ESCALATION]" prefix
4. If still no response, contact via alternate channel

### 7.2 Emergency Escalation

For critical security issues:
1. Email security@firsttry.run
2. Include "[SECURITY]" in subject
3. Provide details and impact assessment

## 8. Customer Responsibilities

To receive timely support, you must:
- Provide detailed issue descriptions
- Include error messages and logs
- Specify App version and Jira version
- Respond to clarifying questions promptly

## 9. Service Exclusions

Support is NOT available for:
- Issues caused by misuse or unauthorized modifications
- Problems outside our control (Forge platform outages)
- Issues in unsupported configurations
- Free trial users (limited support only)

## 10. Limitations and Disclaimers

### 10.1 Best Effort Basis

All support is provided on a **best effort** basis. We do not guarantee:
- Specific resolution times
- Availability of support staff
- Resolution of all issues

### 10.2 Force Majeure

Support may be delayed or unavailable due to:
- Natural disasters
- Internet outages
- Pandemics or public health emergencies
- Other events beyond our control

## 11. SLA Modifications

We reserve the right to modify this SLA with:
- 30 days notice for material changes
- Immediate effect for minor clarifications

## 12. Monitoring and Reporting

### 12.1 Availability Monitoring

We monitor:
- App installation success rates
- Critical error rates
- API response times

### 12.2 Incident Reports

For major incidents, we provide:
- Post-incident summary
- Root cause analysis
- Preventive measures
