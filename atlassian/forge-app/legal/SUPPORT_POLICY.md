# Support Policy

**Effective Date**: 2026-01-10

---

## Support Channels

| Channel | Use For | Response Time |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Email: contact@firsttry.run | Bug reports, security issues, questions | 24 hours |
| GitHub Issues | Feature requests, non-urgent bugs | Best effort |

---

## Support SLAs

- **Critical Issues** (data loss, security): 4-hour response target
- **High Issues** (app unavailable): 24-hour response target
- **Medium Issues** (functionality degraded): 48-hour response target
- **Low Issues** (cosmetic, minor): Best effort

---

## What We Support

- Installation and setup
- Dashboard usage and troubleshooting
- Data export and deletion
- Configuration of governance policies

---

## What We Don't Support

- Jira Cloud administration (beyond FirstTry installation)
- Custom Jira development
- Third-party app integration (outside FirstTry)

---

## Escalation

For urgent issues not resolved within SLA:
1. Email: contact@firsttry.run (mark as URGENT)
2. Include: App version, error logs, steps to reproduce

---

## Product Updates

FirstTry is actively maintained. Updates may include:
- Security patches (high priority)
- Bug fixes
- Feature improvements

We aim to provide backward compatibility for data exports.
