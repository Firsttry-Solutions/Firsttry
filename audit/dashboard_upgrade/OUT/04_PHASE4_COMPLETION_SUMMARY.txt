### Accessibility Assurance

All three components follow strict accessibility guidelines:
- **Icon + Text**: Never color-only rendering
- **ARIA attributes**: role, aria-label, aria-expanded, aria-current, aria-live
- **Semantic HTML**: Proper heading hierarchy, <ol> lists, <button> elements
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Focusable elements, skip links, focus visible states
- **Screen Reader Support**: Meaningful descriptions, live regions for status updates
- **Responsive Design**: Works on all breakpoints (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- **Contrast**: All text meets WCAG AA contrast requirements
